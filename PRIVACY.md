# MultiLaps privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

MultiLaps is a stopwatch app for timing up to four runners at once. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

## What the app stores

MultiLaps keeps three things, and only on your device:

- The names you give the stopwatches.
- The colours you choose for them.
- The current timing: whether each stopwatch is running, and its lap times.

This is kept so that your stopwatches are still there when you reopen the app. It is deleted when you reset the stopwatches, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect personal information.
- It does not have access to the internet. It cannot send anything from your device.
- It has no accounts and no sign-in.
- It has no advertising and no analytics.
- It does not share anything with anyone, because it has nothing to share and no way to send it.
- It does not include its data in device backups or device-to-device transfers.

## Permissions

MultiLaps asks for one permission: to vibrate the device, so that you can feel each press without looking at the screen. It asks for nothing else.

## Children

MultiLaps is meant for the adults who time children's running: parents, carers and coaches. It collects no information from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If a later version of the app changes any of this, this page will be updated, and the date at the top will change.
