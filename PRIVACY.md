# MultiLaps privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

MultiLaps is a stopwatch app for timing up to four runners at once. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

## What the app stores

MultiLaps keeps five things, and only on your device:

- The names you give the stopwatches.
- The colours you choose for them.
- The current timing: whether each stopwatch is running, its lap times, and any note you write on it. Alongside it the app notes when your device was last switched on, which is how a running stopwatch keeps the right time after a restart.
- Your history: each time you reset a stopwatch that has laps, its name, colour, note, lap times and total time are kept as a result, with the date and time of the reset.
- Whether it has already asked Google Play to show its rating card, so that it asks only once (see Rating below).

This is kept so that your stopwatches are still there when you reopen the app, and so that you can look back at earlier times.

Resetting a stopwatch clears its timing, lap times and note from the stopwatch, and keeps its name and colour; the note goes into the result with the laps. A name or colour stays until you change it. A result stays in History until you delete it there, one at a time or all at once; its note can be changed there too. Clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app, deletes all of it.

## Sharing

Each result in History has a Share button, and you can also select several results in the list and share those together. Pressing Share hands the results you chose, as plain text, with any notes on them, ending with a link to MultiLaps on Google Play, to the app you then pick from your phone's share sheet, such as a messaging app. MultiLaps does this only when you press Share, and sends nothing itself. What the app you choose does with the text is up to that app.

## Rating

Once per install (or once again after you clear the app's data), when you leave History at least ten days after installing MultiLaps and no stopwatch is running, the app asks Google Play to show its rating card. The card is Google Play's own: Google Play decides whether to show it, and any rating or review you give there goes to Google Play under its terms. MultiLaps sends nothing with the request and never learns whether you rated it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect personal information.
- It does not have access to the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere itself.
- It has no accounts and no sign-in.
- It has no advertising and no analytics.
- It does not share anything with anyone on its own. The only way anything leaves the app is the Share button described above, when you press it.
- It does not include its data in device backups or device-to-device transfers.

## Permissions

MultiLaps asks for one permission: to vibrate the device, so that you can feel each press without looking at the screen. It asks for nothing else.

## Children

MultiLaps is meant for the adults who time children's running: parents, carers and coaches. It collects no information from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If a later version of the app changes any of this, this page will be updated, and the date at the top will change.
