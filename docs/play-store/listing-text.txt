Release notes for 0.4.0
History: when you reset a stopwatch, its laps are kept. Look back by day, open a result to see every lap, share one or several as text, and swipe left to delete.
Stop is now the same size as Start on every stopwatch.
The app is now a much smaller download, and runs on Android 7.0 and later.
Every press on Lap now counts, however soon after the last.
Shared results end with a link to MultiLaps on Google Play.
Notes: write one on any stopwatch, keep it with the result, and edit it in History.
