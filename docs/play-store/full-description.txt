Two kids on the track and one stopwatch on your phone. You time one of them properly and guess the other.

MultiLaps puts four stopwatches on one screen, so every runner gets their own track stopwatch. It is a multiple stopwatch for anyone timing more than one runner: a parent at the club track, a coach with a small group doing repeats, or whoever has been made sports day timer.

You may have looked for a lap timer, a coach stopwatch or a split timer for runners. It counts up, not down, so here it is called a stopwatch.

Everyone at once
Four stopwatches, each a solid block of colour with the runner's name on it. Start each one when that runner sets off, or press Start all when they go together. Turn the phone on its side and they line up in a row.

Made for the Lap press
Each one is a lap stopwatch first. Lap and Stop are two big buttons at the foot of every stopwatch. Lap is solid and Stop is an outline, so you can tell them apart at a glance. Each press gives a short vibration, different for a lap and a stop, so you can keep your eyes on the track.

The numbers that matter
Each stopwatch shows the lap in progress in large digits, with the last lap, best lap, total time and lap count beneath. Tap a name to see every lap, with the best one marked.

Hard to lose a time
Reset by mistake? Undo it within five seconds. Screen locked, call came in, app closed? The stopwatches are still running when you come back. The screen stays on while anything is being timed.

Look back later
Reset a stopwatch and its laps are kept in History, under the day it started, with the times it started and was reset. Write a note on a stopwatch, such as a windy back straight, and it is kept with the result, to change later if you like. Open a result to see every lap. Share one result as text, or pick several and share them together. Results stay on your phone until you delete them.

Yours to set up
Name each stopwatch and pick its colour from eight. They stay as you set them. Light and dark themes follow your phone.

Nothing in the way
No ads. No account. No internet connection: the app cannot go online, and your times stay on your phone unless you choose to share them.
